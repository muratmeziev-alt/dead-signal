THE GAME

BREACH. LOOT. EXTRACT.

A top-down zombie extraction shooter. You go into a sealed zone, loot what you can carry and try to get out alive. If you die, you lose what you brought in.

BREACH

DEPLOY WITH WHAT YOU CAN CARRY

Leave the bunker with your gear, your rig and nothing to spare. Every room in the zone is a fight: the infected, rival crews and KAZI patrols guarding the trial wards.

LOOT

SEARCH EVERYTHING. CARRY WHAT FITS.

Bodies, crates and lockers all hold something. Every item takes grid space and weight, and every gun you bring home can be stripped and rebuilt on the bench.

EXTRACT

GET OUT, OR LOSE IT ALL

Reach an exit before the signal drops. Make it out and everything you carried is yours. Go down, and it stays where you fell.
